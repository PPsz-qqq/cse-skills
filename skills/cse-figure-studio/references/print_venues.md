## IEEE (Journals and Transactions)

### Width
- **One column**: 3.5 inches / 88.9 millimeters / 21 picas
- **Two columns (full width)**: 7.16 inches / 182 millimeters / 43 picas

**Source**: [IEEE Author Center – Resolution and Size](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-graphics-for-your-article/resolution-and-size/)  
**Quote**: "One column width: 3.5 inches, 88.9 millimeters, or 21 picas"

### Maximum dimensions
- **7.16 × 8.8 inches / 182 × 220 millimeters / 43 × 52 picas** (as stated; note 8.8 in ≠ exactly 220 mm, source rounds inconsistently)

**Source**: [IEEE Author Center – File Formatting](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-graphics-for-your-article/file-formatting/)  
**Quote**: "no larger than 7.16 x 8.8 inches, 182 x 220 millimeters, or 43 x 52 picas"

### Fonts and lettering
- **Typefaces**: Helvetica, Times New Roman, Arial, Cambria, Symbol
- **Size**: Approximately **9–10 point** when viewed at full size
- **Embedding**: All fonts must be embedded or subset

**Sources**:
- [File Formatting](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-graphics-for-your-article/file-formatting/): "Type should appear approximately 9-10 point when viewed at full size."
- [IEEE Xplore Requirements](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/write-your-paper/meet-ieee-xplore-requirements/): "All fonts are embedded or subset."

### Resolution
- **Color and grayscale images**: >300 dpi
- **Black-and-white line art**: >600 dpi
- **Combination art**: Not separately stated on checked Author Center pages

**Source**: [Resolution and Size](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-graphics-for-your-article/resolution-and-size/)  
**Quote**: "IEEE standards for black and white line art are >600dpi."

### File formats
- **Preferred**: PostScript (PS), Encapsulated PostScript (EPS), PDF, PNG, TIFF
- **Original Office drawings accepted**: Word, Excel, PowerPoint
- **JPEG**: Acceptable **for author photos only**
- **Not processed**: Visio (VSD), GIF, BMP

**Source**: [File Formatting](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-graphics-for-your-article/file-formatting/)  
**Quote**: "High-resolution JPEG files are acceptable for author photos only."

### Color
- **Accessibility**: Check that figures are interpretable in grayscale
- **Redundancy**: Use color plus shape, line styles, or patterns
- **Brightness**: Distinguish elements by brightness, not hue alone

**Source**: [Create Graphics](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-graphics-for-your-article/)  
**Quote**: "Use both color and shape to convey the same meaning"

### Type 3 fonts
- **Generic IEEE PDF eXpress**: Checked page does not state whether Type 3 fonts are permitted or prohibited
- **Venue-specific example (iWAT 2024)**: "Type 3 fonts (bitmaps) will not be accepted."

**Interpretation**: Type 3 prohibition is venue-specific, not a universal IEEE rule. Check individual conference/journal instructions.

**Sources**:
- [IEEE Xplore Requirements](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/write-your-paper/meet-ieee-xplore-requirements/): Type 3 not mentioned
- [iWAT 2024 Paper Submission](https://attend.ieee.org/iwat-2024/paper-submission/): "Type 3 fonts (bitmaps) will not be accepted."

## Elsevier

### Width
- **Minimal size**: 30 mm (85 pt)
- **Single column**: 90 mm (255 pt)
- **1.5 column**: 140 mm (397 pt)
- **Double column (full width)**: 190 mm (539 pt)

**Source**: [Elsevier Artwork Sizing](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-sizing)  
**Quote**: Table excerpt: "Minimal size … 30 mm … Single column … 90 mm … 1.5 column … 140 mm … Double column … 190 mm"

### Lettering
- **Normal text**: Finished, printed size of **7 pt**
- **Subscripts and superscripts**: No smaller than **6 pt**
- **Guidance**: "This is a rule-of-thumb rather than a strict rule." Sometimes approximately 10 pt may be appropriate.

**Source**: [Artwork Sizing](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-sizing)  
**Quote**: "This is a rule-of-thumb rather than a strict rule."

### Resolution
- **Line art**: 1000 DPI
- **Halftone images**: 300 DPI
- **Combination art**: 500 DPI

**Source**: [Artwork Sizing](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-sizing)  
**Quote**: "300 DPI for halftone images; 500 DPI for combination art; 1000 DPI for line art."

**Note**: The [Artwork Overview](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-overview) page states "graphs 1000 dpi" and "others 500 dpi" in the TIFF export section, which differs from the Sizing page's explicit "halftone 300 dpi." Use the Sizing page's explicit values.

### File formats
- **Recommended**: EPS, PDF, TIFF, JPEG
- **Accepted**: Microsoft Office files (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)

**Source**: [Artwork Overview](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-overview)  
**Quote**: "Microsoft Office files (Word, Excel and PowerPoint) are also accepted."

### Fonts
- **Typefaces**: Arial (or Helvetica), Courier, Symbol, Times (or Times New Roman)
- **Type**: Type 1 or TrueType where possible
- **Embedding**: Embed all fonts in EPS exports

**Source**: [Artwork Overview](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-overview)  
**Quote**: "only use the following recommended fonts ('Type 1' or 'TrueType')"

### Color space
- **Preferred**: RGB (unless the journal's Guide for Authors prescribes otherwise)

**Source**: [Artwork Formats Checklist](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-formats-checklist)  
**Quote**: "My color images are provided in the preferred RGB colorspace (unless the journal's guide for authors prescribes otherwise)"

## Springer

**Important**: The same official page contains different specifications in its German and English sections. Always check the journal's specific instructions.

**Source**: [Springer Journal 42278 Submission Guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/42278/submission-guidelines) (fetched via Python urllib; web_fetch blocked by cross-origin redirect)

### Width (German section: "Abbildungsgröße")
**Large-format journals**:
- **39 mm, 84 mm, 129 mm, or 174 mm** wide
- Maximum **234 mm** height

**Small-format journals**:
- **80 mm or 122 mm** wide
- Maximum **198 mm** height

**Source quote**: "Für großformatige Zeitschriften gelten die Maße: 39 mm, 84 mm, 129 mm, oder 174 mm breit und nicht höher als 234 mm."

### Width (English section: "Figure Placement and Size")
**Large-format journals**:
- **84 mm** for double-column text areas or **174 mm** for single-column text areas
- Maximum **234 mm** height

**Small-format journals**:
- **119 mm** wide
- Maximum **195 mm** height

**Source quote**: "For small-sized journals, the figures should be 119 mm wide and not higher than 195 mm."

### Lettering
- **Typefaces**: Helvetica or Arial
- **Size**: Usually about **2–3 mm (8–12 pt)** at final size
- **Consistency**: Keep lettering consistently sized throughout the artwork

**Source quote**: "Keep lettering consistently sized throughout your final-sized artwork, usually about 2–3 mm (8–12 pt)."

### Line weight
- At least **0.1 mm (0.3 pt)** wide

**Source quote**: "All lines should be at least 0.1 mm (0.3 pt) wide."

### Resolution
- **Scanned/bitmap line drawings**: Minimum **1200 dpi**
- **Halftones**: Minimum **300 dpi**
- **Combination artwork**: Minimum **600 dpi**

**Source quote**: "Scanned line drawings and line drawings in bitmap format should have a minimum resolution of 1200 dpi."

### File formats
- **Vector graphics**: EPS
- **Halftones**: TIFF
- **Office files**: Accepted
- **Color space**: RGB, 8 bits per channel
- **Fonts**: Embed all fonts in vector graphics

## Conference Proceedings

### CVPR 2026

**LaTeX class dimensions** (derived from official style file):
- `\textwidth`: 6.875 inches (174.625 mm)
- `\columnsep`: 0.3125 inches (7.9375 mm)
- **Derived column width**: 3.28125 inches (83.34375 mm)

**Source**: [Official CVPR Author Kit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cvpr-org/author-kit/main/cvpr.sty)  
**Exact code**: `\setlength{\textwidth}{6.875in}`; `\setlength{\columnsep}{0.3125in}`

**Note**: Font sizes, resolution, and format requirements not stated in checked style file.

### NeurIPS 2026

**LaTeX class dimensions**:
- `textwidth`: 5.5 inches (139.7 mm)
- **Single-column article kit**: column width equals text width

**Source**: [Official NeurIPS 2026 Kit](https://media.neurips.cc/Conferences/NeurIPS2026/Formatting_Instructions_For_NeurIPS_2026.zip)  
**Exact code**: `\newgeometry{ textheight=9in, textwidth=5.5in, top=1in, headheight=12pt, ...}`

**Note**: Font sizes, resolution, and format requirements not stated in checked kit.

### ICML 2026

**LaTeX class dimensions**:
- `\textwidth`: 6.75 inches (171.45 mm)
- `\columnsep`: 0.25 inches (6.35 mm)
- **Derived column width**: 3.25 inches (82.55 mm)

**Source**: [Official ICML 2026 Kit](https://media.icml.cc/Conferences/ICML2026/Styles/icml2026.zip)  
**Exact code**: `\setlength\textwidth{6.75in}`; `\setlength\columnsep{0.25in}`

**Important note**: Official author instructions state: "This year, there is no Type 3 font check"  
**Source**: [ICML 2026 Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorInstructions)

### IEEE Conference Proceedings (IEEEtran class)

**Standard two-column conference settings** (derived from IEEEtran.cls):
- `\textwidth`: 43 picas
- `\columnsep`: 1 pica
- **Column width**: 21 picas
- **TeX conversion**: 1 pc = 12 pt = ~4.2175 mm
- **Approximate dimensions**: Column ~88.57 mm, full width ~181.35 mm

**Source**: [IEEE-hosted historical V1.6](https://ewh.ieee.org/conf/ijcnn/2003/IEEEtran.cls) and [CTAN reference V1.8b](https://mirror.clarkson.edu/ctan/macros/latex/contrib/IEEEtran/IEEEtran.cls)  
**Exact code**: `\textwidth 43pc % 2 x 21pc + 1pc = 43pc`; `\columnsep 1pc`

**Note**: These are class-file defaults. Current ICRA/IROS/CDC author kits were not readable (HTTP 202 empty response). The derived pica values (~88.57/181.35 mm) do not match IEEE Author Center's stated journal dimensions (88.9/182 mm exactly). Specific conference kits may override class defaults.

## Summary of Common Contradictions

### Contradicted blanket claims observed in informal sources

1. **"Chinese is prohibited in all journal figures"**  
   Contradicted by: 自动化学报 explicitly specifies Chinese 宋体 and includes MATLAB conversion steps to preserve Chinese text. 控制理论与应用 Chinese-article template requires Chinese explanations.

2. **"All IEEE venues ban Type 3 fonts"**  
   Contradicted by: Generic IEEE PDF eXpress requirements page does not mention Type 3. Prohibition is venue-specific (e.g., iWAT 2024 explicitly bans; ICML 2026 explicitly has no Type 3 check).

3. **"Elsevier requires exactly 7 pt labels"**  
   Contradicted by: Official sizing page states "This is a rule-of-thumb rather than a strict rule." 7 pt is for normal text; sometimes ~10 pt may be appropriate.

4. **"IEEE figures must be exactly 88.9 mm or 182 mm"**  
   Partial truth: Those are the stated journal column widths. IEEEtran class uses 21/43 picas (~88.57/181.35 mm). Springer offers 4+ width options for large-format journals.

5. **"All academic figures must be 300 dpi"**  
   Contradicted by: IEEE line art >600 dpi; Elsevier line art 1000 dpi; Springer line art ≥1200 dpi; 自动化学报 600 ppi. 300 dpi is typical for halftones/photos, not line art.

6. **"JPEG is acceptable for scientific plots"**  
   Contradicted by: IEEE explicitly limits JPEG to author photos only. Lossy compression creates artifacts in line art and text.

### Internal discrepancies in official sources

1. **Springer bilingual page**: German section lists large-format widths as 39/84/129/174 mm; English section lists 84/174 mm. Small-format: German 80/122 mm; English 119 mm.

2. **Elsevier resolution**: Sizing page states halftones 300 dpi; Overview page's TIFF paragraph says "others 500 dpi."

3. **IEEE dimensions**: Author Center states 7.16 in = 182 mm (true); 8.8 in = 220 mm (false; actually ~223.52 mm). Source rounds inconsistently.

4. **IEEE picas vs millimeters**: IEEEtran class 21 pc ≈ 88.57 mm, not the Author Center's stated 88.9 mm. 43 pc ≈ 181.35 mm, not 182 mm.
